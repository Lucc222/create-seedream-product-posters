## 1. Usage contract

- Select exactly one primary recipe. Mix at most 20% of a second recipe and name which devices are borrowed. Use `R00-custom-family` when the base style atlas fits the product but none of R01–R18 does; do not force a mismatched recipe.
- Preserve the uploaded product exactly. Change scene, lighting, framing, and poster graphics; do not redesign the product.
- Treat every bracketed copy slot as empty until exact copy is supplied or safely written from visible facts.
- Prices, discounts, dates, specifications, ingredients, nutrition, performance, endorsements, delivery promises, warranties, and live-view counts require user-supplied verification.
- A publicity poster has no CTA. Replace the recipe's button zone with a non-interactive slogan, material close, scene continuation, or verified event line.
- For English output, translate the hierarchy and typographic personality, not Chinese wording. Replace Song/calligraphic display type with an appropriate high-contrast serif or expressive Latin display face.
- Never generate placeholder text, fake logos, fake years, `AI生成`, random initials, QR codes, watermarks, or unlisted microcopy.
- Do not copy a third-party brand's distinctive logo, mascot, trade dress, or campaign slogan. Use authorized uploaded assets only.

## 3. R01 东方新奢茶室风

- **Visual thesis:** Make the product a contemporary cultural exhibit inside a warm tea-room ritual.
- **Scene:** dark wood table, moon gate, bamboo shadow, lattice, porcelain, restrained lantern or gift-box depth.
- **Palette/light:** warm rice gold, tea brown, ink green, black-gold; late-afternoon side light, soft haze, realistic wood reflection.
- **Composition/type:** calm central or 55/45 balance; large calligraphic or Song-style headline, fine serif support, framed proof modules, thin key border.
- **Hero event/evidence:** bamboo light passes across the product; add one honest packaging, ingredient, craftsmanship, or serving detail only when visible/supplied.
- **Close:** publicity uses a dark-green plaque-like slogan without arrow; conversion may use a plaque CTA only when requested.
- **Avoid:** fake history, fake tea origin, generic gold overload, unsupported ingredient and craft claims.

## 4. R02 热带海滨清爽饮品风

- **Visual thesis:** Let the drink create the summer atmosphere rather than merely stand in front of a beach.
- **Scene:** bright island horizon, white mineral podium, palm framing, one truthful coconut/fruit cue, ice foreground and refracted sunlight.
- **Palette/light:** cobalt, sky blue, high white, milk ivory, small caramel accent; high-key direct sun and crisp condensation.
- **Composition/type:** hero cup slightly right and 50–65% height; energetic geometric headline with cropped/stacked rhythm; side proof rail and seasonal seal.
- **Hero event/evidence:** controlled splash/refraction wraps behind the cup; one macro ice, condensation, or liquid-layer window.
- **Close:** scene-integrated summer slogan for publicity; price, delivery, newcomer, or CTA modules only from verified campaign input.
- **Avoid:** random tropical props, impossible liquid, splashes over labels, invented flavor or nutrition claims.

## 5. R03 黑金奢华科技发布风

- **Visual thesis:** Present the device as a monumental precision object at a flagship launch.
- **Scene:** near-black studio, black rock, architectural metal rings, vertical champagne light columns, mirror stage and restrained smoke.
- **Palette/light:** black, titanium, champagne gold, warm white; precise rim light, controlled star highlights, no cheap yellow.
- **Composition/type:** one or two source-supported product views in offset three-quarter overlap; oversized beveled metallic headline; disciplined hexagonal proof modules.
- **Hero event/evidence:** product crosses a luminous horizon or engineered aperture; use a material macro or visible construction callout.
- **Close:** launch statement or verified reservation CTA; keep the stage visible through the closing band.
- **Avoid:** invented secondary product, fake specifications, dense HUD wallpaper, random circuitry.

## 6. R04 奶油金东方美妆风

- **Close:** quiet collection line for publicity; soft rounded CTA only for conversion.

## 7. R05 荧光黄机能运动风

- **Visual thesis:** Freeze the product sharply while the city and typography accelerate around it.
- **Scene:** glass city, wet street, runner/action silhouette only when authorized, lane marks and reflective asphalt.

## 8. R06 白桃粉晶莹汽水风

- **Visual thesis:** Build a crystalline fruit-and-bubble world whose physics originate from the bottle.
- **Scene:** transparent acrylic plinth, clear ice, one fruit family, bubbles, glass geometry and a splash crown behind the product.
- **Palette/light:** bright white, pale peach, pink, small magenta; translucent high-key lighting and sharp water detail.
- **Composition/type:** bottle centered with strong silhouette; large pink/black display headline with white keyline, liquid highlight and handwritten underline.
- **Hero event/evidence:** condensation, visible liquid gradient and plausible bubbles; fruit appears only when flavor is verified by label or brief.
- **Close:** publicity uses a clean flavor/season slogan; price, shipping, damage protection and CTA require verified copy.
- **Avoid:** invented fruit, false zero-sugar claims, label-obscuring splash, jelly-like product deformation.

## 11. R09 红黑霓虹超级大促风

- **Scene:** black-red future sale hall, concentric stage, red light bands, metal depth and product pyramid.
- **Palette/light:** campaign red, black, high white, restrained warm gold; glossy stage reflections.
- **Composition/type:** platform/date ownership, giant 3D campaign title, offer banner, 3–7 SKU pyramid, deal bar, logistics bar and trust rail.

## 14. R12 暖阳家庭早餐营养风

## 20. R18 空灵新中式茶空间风

- **Visual thesis:** Make contemporary tea a spatial and social brand experience with calm modern Oriental depth.
- **Scene:** low round wood plinth, huge moon window, bamboo intrusion, misty ink landscape and softly blurred social tea-space figures when authorized.
- **Palette/light:** rice white, natural wood, bamboo green, smoke gray and small authorized red; low saturation and natural window light.
- **Composition/type:** generous negative space, centered or gently asymmetric product, wide-tracked Song/high-contrast serif title and four thin-line image pillars.
- **Hero event/evidence:** moon-window geometry echoes the product; bamboo shadow and mist frame one visible cup/pack material detail.
- **Close:** wide-tracked brand declaration; no price or action button.
- **Avoid:** generic empty tea room, fake milk/tea ingredients, fake space/service claims, costume-drama clutter.

## 21. Shared negative controls

Use these in addition to category-specific truth locks:

```text
No low resolution, blur, compression noise, overexposure, dirty or accidental background,
cheap plastic material, deformed product, redesigned package, wrong cap/lid, twisted cup rim,
deformed wheels or sole, incorrect product count, duplicate hero product, distorted hands or face,
wrong perspective, floating debris without purpose, muddy color, contradictory shadow directions,
smeared texture, illegible or unlisted text, wrong logo, fake trademark, watermark, QR code,
subject crop, chaotic hierarchy, generic triptych, unsupported claims, fake offer or fake specification.
Preserve the uploaded product's shape, structure, color, proportion, label placement, and visible details;
change only the scene, lighting, composition, and authorized poster graphics.
```
